FE pass-score myths checklist

NCEES does not publish a raw cut score. Track weak areas and pacing instead of chasing a magic number.

Myth: “You need 70%.”
Reality: There is no published percentage. Read what score do you need to pass the FE?
Myth: “It is curved against your cohort.”
Reality: Scaling absorbs form difficulty, not peer ranking. See pass rates context.
Myth: “Results take months.”
Reality: CBT results are typically released on a published cadence — FE exam results timing.
Better metric: After each mock, list time sinks and topic misses, then drill with worked practice problems and a timed free mock.
